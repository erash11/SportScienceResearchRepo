## Decision Notes

1. **90-minute nap timing:** Include as a small randomized crossover study; the 13:00 nap outperformed later or no-nap conditions on selected outcomes, not every measure.
2. **Football preseason load and recovery:** Include as a 14-day cohort showing that session RPE, mechanical load, reactive strength, and perceived recovery provide different signals.
3. **Neck strength and injury:** Include as a one-team exploratory cohort; reported strength cut points require external validation and do not prove prevention.
4. **Soccer worst-case scenarios:** Include as one-team monitoring evidence supporting local rolling peak-demand profiles rather than universal values.
5. **Targeted soccer preseason program:** Include as a small randomized bundled intervention with sprint and change-of-direction benefits but no prospective injury outcome.
6. **ACL return and career:** Include because high return rates concealed lower same-level return, performance, and career survival compared with matched controls.
7. **Gluteal EMG and muscle force:** Include as laboratory evidence warning against ranking biomechanically different exercises by peak EMG alone.
8. **Blood-flow restriction application:** Include as a narrative methods review emphasizing individualized cuff and pressure decisions under trained oversight.
9. **Copenhagen plus Nordic exercises:** Include as an uncontrolled four-week strength study without demonstrated jump, sprint, or injury-prevention transfer.
10. **COVID-19 in athletes:** Include as a systematic review showing mostly mild acute illness, a minority with persistent symptoms, and uncertain causal cardiac findings.
11. **Cluster and rest-redistribution sets:** Include as a meta-analysis of acute fatigue management; rest structure and total rest complicate comparisons and chronic transfer remains uncertain.
12. **Overspeed towing:** Include as a meta-analysis of acute assisted sprint responses without evidence that faster assisted trials produce retained unassisted speed.

## Verification Verdict

- **Correctness: PASS** — source identities, DOI metadata, designs, sample sizes, quantitative findings, null findings, and limitations were checked against each full text. The blood-flow-restriction DOI absent from extracted PDF text was cross-checked against the publication metadata record.
- **Quality: PASS** — practical translations distinguish acute response from adaptation, association from causation, monitoring description from prescription, and binary return from restored performance.
