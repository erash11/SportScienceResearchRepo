## Decision Notes

1. **Complementary-football analytics:** Include as a methodological example. Reverse causality remained and added adjustment did not clearly improve prediction beyond schedule and home field.
2. **Football CMJ positions:** Positional movement strategies and momentum differed, but single-program group means are not fatigue or talent thresholds.
3. **Neck extension and concussion:** Extension strength associated with lower concussion rate; the cohort does not prove a strengthening intervention prevents concussion.
4. **Mexican LFA morphology:** Valuable population-specific norms with only 57.7% exploratory position classification and no performance validation.
5. **Previous injury and CMJ asymmetry:** Phase-specific asymmetries persisted despite equal jump outcomes; injury types were pooled and prospective relevance is unknown.
6. **Professional-rugby cervical profile:** Position, age, mass, and individual variability matter; norms are not protection or return-to-contact cutoffs.
7. **Chronic ankle instability:** Most primary studies did not define CAI adequately, so supported deficits apply more safely to nonspecific instability.
8. **Foam rolling:** Repeated use improved ROM on average, but effects were joint-specific and did not establish performance or clinical benefit.
9. **Beer and exercise:** Low-alcohol and nonalcoholic findings do not justify alcoholic beer as recovery; small male samples and artificial intake protocols dominate.
10. **Head, face, and neck cooling:** Comfort improved while core-temperature effects were negligible and performance remained inconclusive.
11. **Hydration, heat, and glycogen:** Heat showed a clearer adverse glycogen signal than hydration alone; estimates were small, old, and heterogeneous.
12. **Intermittent fasting:** Performance was often maintained, but fasting format, energy intake, sleep, hydration, and Ramadan context were confounded.
