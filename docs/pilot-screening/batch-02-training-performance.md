## Material Taxonomy Corrections

- Inter-limb asymmetry moved to **Monitoring & Technology** because its primary question is measurement instability across test and load conditions.
- Hip strength and future groin pain, implementation of the Adductor Strengthening Programme, and the preventive cluster trial moved to **Sports Medicine & Injury**.
- Fixture congestion, acute load and muscle-damage responses, and elite-athlete sleep moved to **Recovery & Readiness**.
- Cannabidiol and endurance-exercise responses moved to **Nutrition & Hydration**.
- Achilles rupture career and performance outcomes moved to **Rehabilitation & Return to Sport**.

## Decision Notes

1. **Inter-limb asymmetry:** Include as test-selection evidence; asymmetry magnitude and direction were load dependent.
2. **Hip strength and future groin pain:** Include with low-certainty prognostic framing; abductor strength and strength ratio were not predictive.
3. **Fixture congestion:** Include for schedule planning; unchanged total distance does not demonstrate complete recovery.
4. **Rugby-league training load:** Include as a heterogeneous dose-response review without universal load thresholds.
5. **Soccer off-season:** Include for maintenance planning; structured training attenuated but did not eliminate detraining.
6. **Programme implementation:** Include as a fidelity warning; high adoption coexisted with frequent protocol modification.
7. **Blood-flow restriction:** Include as a small healthy-adult strength trial, not injured-athlete safety proof.
8. **Adductor prevention trial:** Include with the studied progression and population attached to the 41% relative reduction.
9. **Cannabidiol:** Include as a null acute trial; 150 mg did not improve subjective response or 10 km performance.
10. **Acute load and muscle damage:** Include with biomarker caution; creatine kinase is not a standalone readiness measure.
11. **Achilles rupture:** Include for professional-sport expectations; 76% returned at an average of 11 months with persistent performance concerns.
12. **Elite-athlete sleep:** Include for schedule and travel planning; observational group effects are not individual sleep prescriptions.
