## Decision Notes

1. **Football autonomic recovery:** High-intensity duration related to next-day cardiac recovery in one male Division I team; the observational data do not establish a universal threshold or full recovery time.
2. **Substance use in elite athletes:** Include for wellbeing and clinical context. Estimates were heterogeneous, diagnosed disorder prevalence was absent, and sport or substance stereotypes are unsafe.
3. **Rugby duty of care:** Retired players described pressure, test manipulation, and a preference for independent medical decisions; qualitative accounts inform safeguards but not prevalence.
4. **Soccer training-to-match ratios:** Weekly load patterns associated with result in one team, but opponent, tactics, selection, and other confounding prevent causal claims about winning.
5. **Reactive strength after ACLR:** Rebound deficits remained after rehabilitation and support a broader battery; a cross-sectional symmetry result is not a stand-alone clearance rule.
6. **Match intensity distribution:** Average locomotor load substantially underrepresented short peaks, but match peaks also do not equal individual maximum capacity.
7. **Additive manufacturing:** Some products outperformed conventional comparators, but evidence was sparse, poorly replicated, and weak on durability and field transfer.
8. **Melatonin:** No pooled ergogenic effect was found. A change in one antioxidant marker does not establish broad recovery benefit or justify unsupervised supplementation.
9. **BFR cuff pressure:** Individualization did not eliminate wide pressure variation or weak rationale. Equipment, occlusion measurement, contraindications, and response must govern practice.
10. **Isometric training:** Muscle length, intent, intensity, and volume shaped different adaptations; limited direct comparisons do not support a single optimal protocol.
11. **Perception-action agility:** Reactive agility improved, but dose values were descriptive rather than proven optima and the evidence was heterogeneous, male-dominant, and sport-concentrated.
12. **Muscle-strain situations:** Video revealed muscle- and sport-specific patterns, primarily in male football and hamstrings; situations are not single causal mechanisms.
