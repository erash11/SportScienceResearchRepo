## Decision Notes

1. **Football strength and power thresholds:** Include as a cross-sectional classification study; roster- and method-specific thresholds are not causal prescriptions.
2. **Basketball sleep extension:** Include as an uncontrolled pre-post study; improved sleep and performance measures cannot be separated fully from time, training, and sleep-debt effects.
3. **Soccer performance in heat:** Include as four-season observational monitoring evidence; mostly trivial-to-small thermal relationships require player and match context.
4. **Professional rugby readiness signals:** Include as a short case series supporting complementary signals rather than a stand-alone neurological or proprietary readiness score.
5. **Sprint Mechanics Assessment Score:** Include as a reliability study; good rater consistency does not establish injury prediction or intervention responsiveness.
6. **InBody 570 versus DXA:** Include because strong correlation coexisted with systematic underestimation, demonstrating that the methods are not interchangeable.
7. **Anabolic-androgenic steroid adverse effects:** Include as pooled multisystem risk evidence while preserving the observational and heterogeneous exposure limitations.
8. **Compression garments during intense exercise:** Include as a meta-analysis showing no reliable acute performance or physiological advantage during exercise.
9. **SARM abuse:** Include as a systematic review of uncertain prevalence, product contamination, possible performance effects, and clinically important safety concerns.
10. **Change-of-direction biomechanics:** Include as a systematic review of angle- and task-specific correlates, not universal causal technique rules.
11. **Blood-flow-restricted knee rehabilitation:** Include as controlled rehabilitation evidence supporting supervised low-load strength work when heavy loading is limited.
12. **Caffeine in intermittent sport:** Include as acute crossover evidence for small-to-moderate improvements in selected competition actions, with individual side-effect and sleep tradeoffs.

## Verification Verdict

- **Correctness: PASS** — source identities, publication metadata, study designs, sample sizes, quantitative findings, null findings, and limitations were checked against each full text. The football threshold DOI was cross-checked against its publication record, and the InBody record explicitly preserves the absence of an assigned DOI.
- **Quality: PASS** — practical translations distinguish correlation from agreement, association from causation, reliability from predictive validity, acute response from adaptation, and monitoring support from clearance or prescription.
